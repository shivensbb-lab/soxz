# Room Walkthrough — Treatment (Tilt-Shift Miniature)

**Film:** *Room 4×6.5* · 36 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · no voice · English signage captions
**Subject:** Shiven's redesigned bedroom: a dark collector's and gaming room, built as a to-scale 3D model of the real room. The ceiling is lifted off, like a dollhouse, and the room is filmed from above through a tilt-shift lens, so it reads as a model on a table.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The relay of rooms.** The tilt-shift band jumps between zones (display, desk, sofa, bed), with a hard cut on every beat. *Against it:* it would read as a product catalogue, with no one in it.
2. **Before/after.** The plain white room as it is now, time-lapsed into the new one as furniture builds itself. *Against it:* half the film would be spent on the old room, which the user doesn't want to see.
3. **One day in the room, dawn to night, with one tiny occupant (chosen).** From a single high vantage, the sun sweeps through the balcony glass while a tiny figure (Shiven) walks the real route: in through the door, past the collection wall, to the desk, the sofa with a friend, and finally bed. Night brings on the amber lights. *Why:* it is a walkthrough with a reason. The route proves the circulation, the light proves the mood, and the ending drops into the floor plan the user asked for.

**Opening:** mid-time-lapse at dawn. Sunlight rakes in from the balcony and the sharp band is already on the empty doorway.
**Ending:** the camera rises to straight top-down, the lens un-tilts (the band widens to full sharpness) and the model becomes the labelled floor plan, held 5 s.
**Score shape:** a pulse that adds voices one at a time, a tape-stop into silence for the real-time moment, then a music-box version of the theme over the plan.

## 2. Logline and arc
A tiny figure lives one sped-up day in a model of his own room, until night falls, time stops on the thing he loves most, and the room turns into its own blueprint.
- **Setup:** dawn, empty room, the door opens.
- **Turn:** night. The amber lights click on one by one, and time crashes to ×1 at the collection wall.
- **Ending:** rise, un-tilt, floor plan.

## 3. Benchmark
- **Tilt-shift time-lapse photography of interiors and dollhouse cutaways** (architectural cutaway models). *Learn:* the high oblique vantage into a roofless room, the blur band as a pointer, low-rate stutter for people and smooth light. *Don't take:* any specific shot, building or music.
- **Architectural presentation films** (a plan that becomes a space). *Learn:* how a plan is labelled and held long enough to read. *Don't take:* graphics or fonts.

## 5. Beat sheet (100 bpm, beat 0.6 s, bar 2.4 s)
- 0.0 Dawn light at ×120; marimba pulse on its own. Clock reads 06:40 ×120.
- 2.4 The door swings open (woodblock); the figure steps in.
- 7.2 The band follows the figure past the cabinets; glockenspiel joins.
- 12.0 Cut on the beat to the desk track; the figure sits; keyboard clicks gate the vibraphone.
- 16.8 Sunset. 18.0 / 18.6 / 19.2 / 19.8 the four cove-light segments click on, each one a bell chord.
- 20.4 The friend enters; 21.6 both sit; TV flicker.
- 24.0 Tape-stop. 24.6 silence. 25.2 a single glockenspiel note as the cabinet LED glows. Real-time room tone.
- 28.8 Rise begins; music-box theme. 31.2 the plan labels arrive one by one on the beat. 33.6–36.0 hold.

## 8. Captions and titles
The captions are the room's own signage: small dark brass-edged panels with amber text, set in **Overpass** (OFL). They sit in the lower blur band, each held ≥ 2.4 s. A monospace time-lapse readout (`06:40 · ×120 TIME-LAPSE`) sits top-left. The title SHIVEN'S ROOM is laid into the rug in the first shot. The plan labels are set in the same panel type, held ≥ 5 s.

## Note on rights
The film shows no franchise logos, names or characters. The collection is drawn as generic tiny figures, fruit replicas, cards on stands and a hat, and the flag is a generic skull. That keeps the film free of anything the user doesn't own the rights to.
